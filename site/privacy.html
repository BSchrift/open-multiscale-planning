Multiscale

Privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026.

What Multiscale keeps

Your plan: goals, projects, milestones, tasks, practices, time blocks, how they went, and your settings. It lives on your devices, and once you sign in, it is also stored on Amazon Web Services so your devices can sync. Signing in uses your email address and a password.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, Multiscale asks for three permissions and uses them only for these features:

  • To create one calendar named Time blocks and keep it matching your plan's time blocks.
  • To read the list of your calendars and the events in them for the coming weeks, so your plan shows when you are already busy. Those events are copied into your synced plan, visible only to you.

Multiscale never changes or deletes anything in your other calendars. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How the app is running

While you're signed in, the app reports how it is running so problems can be found and fixed: how much memory it uses, memory warnings from your phone, which screen is open, how the last run ended, and the type and place in the app's code of any error, never its message. It also sends your device model, its system version and a random id for this install. None of it includes anything you wrote.

What Multiscale doesn't do

No ads, no trackers, no selling or sharing your data. Nobody reads your plan. Logs record that requests happened, never what your records or events say.

Removing your data

Disconnect Google Calendar in Multiscale's Settings, or remove its access at any time under Third-party apps & services in your Google account. To have your account and everything synced to it deleted, open an issue on the project's GitHub page asking for it, and say nothing private there: we'll follow up to confirm it's you.
