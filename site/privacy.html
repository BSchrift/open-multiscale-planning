Multiscale

Privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026.

What Multiscale keeps

Your plan: goals, projects, milestones, tasks, practices, time blocks, how they went, and your settings. It lives on your devices, and once you sign in, it is also stored on Amazon Web Services so your devices can sync. Signing in uses your email address and a password.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, Multiscale asks for three permissions and uses them only for these features:

  • To create one calendar named Time blocks and keep it matching your plan's time blocks.
  • To read the list of your calendars and the events in them for the coming weeks, so your plan shows when you are already busy. Those events are copied into your synced plan, visible only to you.

To do this while you're away, Multiscale keeps the access Google gives it for your account, encrypted so that it works only for you, and a hashed form of your Google account's address, so that one Google account connects to one Multiscale account. It never shows them to anyone, your devices included. Both are deleted when you disconnect and when your account is deleted, and disconnecting or deleting your account also tells Google to end that access. If you remove Multiscale's access in Google, Multiscale stops using them, and deletes them when you connect again or disconnect.

Multiscale never changes or deletes anything in your other calendars. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How the app is running

While you're signed in, the app reports how it is running so problems can be found and fixed: how much memory it uses, memory warnings from your phone, which screen is open, how the last run ended, and the type and place in the app's code of any error, never its message. It also sends your device model, its system version and a random id for this install. None of it includes anything you wrote.

What Multiscale doesn't do

No ads, no trackers, no selling or sharing your data. Nobody reads your plan. Logs record that requests happened, never what your records or events say.

Your data, and removing it

Export my data, in Multiscale's Settings under Account, downloads everything your account holds as a zip: every record as JSON, your notes as Markdown, and your photos and files, with a short guide to the format. The zip is kept for you to download for up to 2 days, then deleted.

Delete my account and data, beside it, deletes your account and everything synced to it: your plan, notes, photos and files, settings, your Google Calendar connection, whose Time blocks calendar is removed from your Google account, and your sign-in. It asks you to sign in again first, and can't be undone. It happens at once, and every device you're signed in on erases its copy the next time it opens or syncs. A copy of the app from before this update shows it's signed out instead, and keeps its copy until someone signs in on it. Copies in our backups are kept for 35 days, then they're gone too; nothing is restored from them into an account. Request logs, which hold a one-way code for your account and never what you wrote, are kept for 30 days, and the database's record of recent changes for 24 hours.

Disconnect Google Calendar in Multiscale's Settings, or remove its access at any time under Third-party apps & services in your Google account.
